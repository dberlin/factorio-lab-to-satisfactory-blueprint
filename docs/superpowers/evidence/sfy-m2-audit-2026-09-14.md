## The defect this corpus found, and its fix

The first run of this gate (at `c8e149b2`) was **FAIL on one cell**:
`steel-beam-20` in mk3 laid out and the validator rejected it three times.

```
flow.capacity  Build_FoundryMk1_C 2      needs 0.75 items/s of 'iron-ore'  belts bring 0.5833
flow.capacity  Build_FoundryMk1_C 2      needs 0.75 items/s of 'coal'      belts bring 0.5833
flow.capacity  Build_ConstructorMk1_C 19 needs 1 items/s of 'steel-ingot'  belts bring 0.3333
```

The spec is the arithmetic: the foundry group is `count=2 last_clock=7/9` with
`inputs_per_machine={'iron-ore': 3/4, 'coal': 3/4}`, and the constructor group
`count=2 last_clock=1/3` with `{'steel-ingot': 1}`.  `0.75 x 7/9 = 0.5833` and
`1 x 1/3 = 0.3333`, exactly.  The belts feeding the SHARD -- a group's
underclocked last machine -- were sized for the shard's own rate, which is
right; the check compared them against the group's full-clock
`inputs_per_machine`, which is not.  The finding was in the check.

`steel-beam-20` is the first corpus entry to reach it: every flow captured
before this one has whole machine counts at clock 1, so no fixture had a shard
on a belt at all.  Its FactorioLab flow asks for `16/9` foundries and `4/3`
constructors and produces two shards in one build.

Fixed at `729a7e81`, "Feed the last machine of a row at its own clock, and
report what cannot be measured", and the cell is CLEAN above.

## What refuses, and what that does and does not mean

32 refusals, all ruled: 14 on depth, 15 on width, 3 on `fluids are M4`.  The
two shapes mean different things and a reader should not merge them.

* **Depth** is the real M2 ceiling.  R10 lays one level of rows inside one
  Blueprint Designer, and `n rows want N cm of band` against 3200 / 4000 / 4800
  is that ruling being applied.  `screw-120` at three rows and 6200 cm is the
  smallest chain no mark holds.
* **Width is a missing feature, not a wall.**  M2 lays each recipe group out as
  ONE unbroken row, so a group wider than the mark refuses even where the same
  machines would sit on the floor perfectly well split across two rows.  That
  split is spec 9.1, now Task 8c on the M2 plan (`d52ff99b`).  Until it lands, a
  width refusal says "M2 has no shape for this yet".

The measured ceiling today is **two rows in an mk3**: all four CLEAN cells are
two-row chains in the largest designer.

## What this says about the M2 ceiling

Depth is now the ONLY shape of geometric refusal in the corpus, and it is R10's
one level of rows meeting the designer's own floor: 28 cells want more band than
their mark has. Two levers remain, and neither is Task 8c's:

* **fewer rows** -- a row per recipe group is the model; a build that put two
  groups on one band, or that stacked, would need a different one (R10 rules
  stacking out for M2);
* **shallower rows** -- a row is 1600 cm because its chains clear the whole
  machine line and its merger chain stands clear of the machines' boxes. Every
  one of those numbers is the registry's, so the lever is the layout rule and not
  the arithmetic.

The measured ceiling is still **two rows in an mk3**, with one three-row-equivalent
exception: `concrete-60` in an mk2 is two rows of one group.
