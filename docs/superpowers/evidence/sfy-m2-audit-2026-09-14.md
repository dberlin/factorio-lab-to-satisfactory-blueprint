## The one miss: `flow.capacity` does not read `last_clock`

`steel-beam-20` in mk3 lays out, and the validator then rejects it three times:

```
flow.capacity  Build_FoundryMk1_C 2      needs 0.75 items/s of 'iron-ore'     and the belts bring 0.5833
flow.capacity  Build_FoundryMk1_C 2      needs 0.75 items/s of 'coal'         and the belts bring 0.5833
flow.capacity  Build_ConstructorMk1_C 19 needs 1 items/s of 'steel-ingot'     and the belts bring 0.3333
```

The spec is the arithmetic:

```
steel-ingot  foundry      count=2  clock=1  last_clock=7/9  inputs_per_machine={'iron-ore': 3/4, 'coal': 3/4}
steel-beam   constructor  count=2  clock=1  last_clock=1/3  inputs_per_machine={'steel-ingot': 1}
```

`0.75 x 7/9 = 0.5833` and `1 x 1/3 = 0.3333`, exactly.  The belts feeding the
SHARD -- the underclocked last machine of a group -- are sized for the shard's
own rate, which is right; the check compares them against the group's
full-clock `inputs_per_machine`, which is not.  The finding is in the check,
not in the layout.

This is the first corpus entry to reach it.  Every flow the earlier tasks
captured has whole machine counts at clock 1 (`iron-plate-60`'s manifest note
says so), so no fixture until now had a shard on a belt at all;
`steel-beam-20`'s FactorioLab flow asks for `16/9` foundries and `4/3`
constructors and produces two shards in one build.

It is not fixed here: `src/flab2bp/sfy/layout/validate.py` was out of this
task's scope and under another agent's hand at the time.  The defect reproduces
on the working tree as well as on the export, so it is not Batch D's.
